Privacy Policy for Social Media Redirector

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Data Collection

Social Media Redirector does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The extension operates entirely locally within your browser.

Permissions Used

PermissionPurpose
declarativeNetRequest / declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccessRewrite URLs locally via browser-built-in DNR rules — no network requests are intercepted or inspected by the extension.
storageSave your per-platform toggles and the front-end instance you picked, locally. No data is synced or sent externally.
webNavigationDetect Instagram and X login redirects (?next=, ?redirect_after_login=), notice when a supported page moves to another URL inside the same tab (so it can be redirected immediately instead of a second later), and remember which tab is on a supported site so turning a toggle on can redirect that tab.

What the Extension Does

  • When enabled, it redirects your browser from supported social media domains to their privacy-respecting front-end alternatives.
  • All redirect logic runs inside your browser via chrome.declarativeNetRequest rules and a service worker.
  • The extension never sends your browsing data, URLs, or any other information to any remote server.

Third-Party Services

The extension redirects you to third-party front-ends:

  • kittygram instances (Instagram alternative, default kittygram.irelephant.net)
  • shitter (nitter fork) instances for X — default shitter.thepixora.com
  • exporttok.com, tikvib.com or viewtikstories.com (TikTok alternatives, one selected by you)
  • shoelace.mint.lgbt (Threads alternative)

X and Instagram can be pointed at a different instance by entering its host in the popup. That hostname is stored locally in your browser and only decides where your requests go for that platform — it is never sent to us.

These services are operated independently. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for information on how they handle your data.

Website

This landing page is served by GitHub Pages. It runs no analytics or tracking scripts and sets no cookies. One request goes to api.github.com to read the repository's star count, which shares only your IP address with GitHub, as any web request does.
